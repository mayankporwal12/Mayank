QYRON AI's tagline is “Your Personal Intelligence.” The user wants a simple, dark, mobile-friendly chatbot that works well on Android and feels welcoming to Hindi and Hinglish speakers.

The local demo should route English, Hindi, and Hinglish prompts for general questions, Class 12 study, maths, physics, chemistry, coding, business ideas, and casual conversation. Keep that demo available without a provider key. If live AI is enabled, send messages through the server and keep the provider key out of frontend code.

**Why:** The user originally requested a free local demo, then asked to prepare an optional secure server-side AI connection while preserving keyless operation.

**How to apply:** Preserve the Android-first Hindi/Hinglish design and label local replies as illustrative. Use live AI only when configured on the server; otherwise keep replies local and do not require a paid service.
