QYRON AI's tagline is “Your Personal Intelligence.” The user wants a simple, dark, mobile-friendly chatbot that works well on Android and feels welcoming to Hindi and Hinglish speakers.

The local demo should route English, Hindi, and Hinglish prompts for general questions, Class 12 study, maths, physics, chemistry, coding, business ideas, and casual conversation. Keep it free and local; do not add or expose an API key or require Replit Core.

**Why:** The user stated these product requirements directly.
